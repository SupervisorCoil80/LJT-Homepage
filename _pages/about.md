I am a first-year PhD candidate at the HKUST NLP Group. I am supervised by Professor Junxian He, who also advised me during my undergraduate studies at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU). My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning.

## Academic Background

- **Ph.D. in Computer Science (2024-Present)**, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology; HKUST NLP Group.
- **B.Eng. (2020-2024)**, Shanghai Jiao Tong University. I graduated from SJTU in June 2024.

## Research Experience

- **MINIMAX** — Research Intern, February 2025 - Present.
- **Tencent WXG** — Research Intern, June 2024 - September 2024; advisor: Zifei Shan.
- **Shanghai AI Lab** — Research Intern, June 2023 - December 2023; advisor: Prof. Yu Cheng.

## Skills and Research Interests

- Natural language processing and machine learning.
- LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning.
- Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM).
- LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability.

## Honors and Awards

- Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship, awarded by Shanghai Jiao Tong University.
